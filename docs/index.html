Kotatsu — a comfortable launcher.

A separately installable fork of Katapult by gezimos, with optional setup, a familiar app grid and customizable home shortcuts. Its Kotatsu emblem and Lato wordmark identify the fork.

Kotatsu has no built-in updater or Internet permission. External browser/document-provider/Mudita weather handoffs and Android-managed backup/device transfer remain and can network independently. The demo, device mockups and screenshots below are upstream Katapult references, not captures of the current Kotatsu artwork.

Setup

How to install

Build Kotatsu from the source repository. Upstream Katapult releases are separate distributions, not Kotatsu updates. Final release/signing and device acceptance are separate verification gates.

How to access Settings

Long-press on any empty area of the home screen.

How to replace home apps

Long-press on the Phone, SMS, or extra row shortcuts, then pick the app you want to assign.

Clock / Date shortcut

Long-press on the clock or date to assign an app to it.

Features

App Shortcuts

Shortcuts like New timer get their own tiles. Rename, re-icon, or assign to any home slot. Pinned websites too.

Dark Mode

White-on-black across the whole launcher, monochrome icons included.

Text Backgrounds

White islands behind clock, date, and app names keep text legible over any wallpaper.

Lockscreen Notifications

Experimental: per-app notification counts on the lock screen. Movable and resizable.

Music Widget

Playback controls with song info. Works with Auxio, Spotify, Fossify Music, Foobar, and more.

Icon Tinting & Shaping

Circle or rounded shapes. Uniform black & white across ~95% of apps.

Custom & Material Icons

Use inherited bundled icons, a 133-strong outlined Material set, or your own PNG or SVG.

Notification Badges

Badge counts on app icons. Works with any app that sends notifications. Long-press an app to clear its badge.

Call & SMS Badges

Kompakt only. Missed, declined, and voicemail counts on Phone, unread counts on Messages, matching the stock Mudita launcher.

E-Ink Optimized

Zero animations, instant swaps, screen refresh on home press to clear ghosting.

Extra Dock Row

Optional 3 additional shortcuts above the Phone/SMS bar.

Battery & Clock

Battery percentage with level icon, alarm indicator, selectable clock & date formats.

Simple Wallpaper

Set a background image directly. Not managed by Android.

Hide & Rename Apps

Hide from the context menu or bulk-hide. Rename any app.

Reorder Apps

Swap-based reordering with reset to alphabetical.

Double-Tap Action

Double-tap the background to toggle brightness or lock the phone. Or leave it off.

Hide Status Bar

Remove the Android status bar for a cleaner look, or keep it and hide just its duplicate clock.

Config Backup

Export every setting to a JSON file and restore it later. Foreign or corrupted files are rejected, not applied.

Permissions

Notification Listener

Required to read incoming notifications from Android and display badge counts on app icons. Without this, notification indicators won't work. Grant it in Settings > Notification Access.

System Settings

Optional for the double-tap brightness feature. Kotatsu needs write access to system settings only for that action. Grant it deliberately in Settings > Apps > Kotatsu > Modify System Settings.

Package Access

Required to query all installed apps and display them in the grid. Also used to trigger the Android uninstall dialog from the app context menu.

Call Log & SMS (Mudita Kompakt only)

Optional on supported Mudita Kompakt devices. Kotatsu requests SMS-read and call-log-read access separately for badge counts; neither is required to finish setup. Call-log write access is a separate deliberate acknowledgment action, not part of initial read setup.

Accessibility Service (optional features only)

Listed as Kotatsu Action Service. This optional inherited service supports lockscreen overlays, clock cover, locking and screensaver features. Enable it deliberately only for features you choose; it is not needed for ordinary launcher preview use.

FAQ

Will it have customizable fonts?

No.

Will it have a customizable grid?

No.

Will it have third-party icon packs?

No.

Can I change date/clock formats?

Yes. In Settings, cycle Clock Format (System, 24-hour, or 12-hour with/without AM/PM) and Date Format (System, weekday, long, numeric, or ISO). Leave them on System to follow Android.

Can I reorder apps?

Yes. Long-press an app, tap Reorder, then tap another app to swap. Tap Save when done.

Will this launcher have a notification tray?

No. No notification tray, no letters screen.

Can it show notifications on the lock screen?

Yes. Enable the experimental Lockscreen Notifications widget under Settings > Lockscreen, then long-press it to move and resize. Full details here.

Notification indicators not showing?

Go to Settings > Notifications > Notification Log and check if the notification exists in Android. Many apps require Google Play Services for push notifications.
