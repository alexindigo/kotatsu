A comfortable launcher.

A little more space for what you use. A little more say in what your phone can access.

Kotatsu is a privacy-focused Android launcher for the Mudita Kompakt e-ink phone. A large clock, a simple app grid and monochrome icons make a calm place to come home to.

Icons without a download

2,174 offline choices from Simple Icons, Tabler and Material. Search the collections, import a PNG or SVG, or reset to default. Reviewed brand matches and hand-picked stock line icons apply automatically; other apps keep their original icons. Your manual choices come first.

Set up at your own pace

Try Kotatsu without making it your default launcher or granting sensitive access. Separate collection and action choices let you control badges, media, usage information, notification dismissal and more, alongside Android's grants.

Keep the app list quiet

Hide, rename and reorder apps. Hidden Apps and Kompakt System Apps have their own controls. Hidden apps stay off the lock screen unless you tick their separate Notify box; lock-screen exclusions still take precedence.

Make it feel like home

Rounded or circle app icons, round page controls, an optional extra dock row and music controls. Choose clock and date formats, light or dark appearance, wallpaper and text backgrounds. Home-button refresh helps clear e-ink ghosting.

A useful lock screen.

The weather where you need it, over the hours that matter.

Kotatsu Weather is a separate, optional app. It uses the network. Add up to four places, each with its own label and hours. Search for a place or request your current location once; choose Celsius or Fahrenheit.

On Kompakt, Kotatsu can host installed Android app widgets in a configurable lock-screen box over the padlock and unlock hint. Add Weather or another installed widget, then choose size and order.

Lock-screen hosting needs the optional Kotatsu Action Service. The setup guide explains how to enable it and leave the floating accessibility shortcut off.

Extra features are choices, not a condition of trying the launcher.

Where are Settings and shortcuts?

Long-press an empty part of Home for Settings. Long-press Phone, SMS or an extra-row shortcut to replace it; long-press the clock or date to assign an app. In All Apps, long-press an app to rename, change its icon, hide or reorder it. Tap another app to swap, then Save. Unsaved exits offer Save, Discard or Cancel.

What does the launcher access?

Kotatsu lists installed packages and can ask Android to uninstall an app. Optional grants support notification badges and media information, system-settings access for brightness, usage access for recent/most-used sorting, and supported Kompakt call-log/SMS reads for Phone/Messages badges. Call-log write access is a separate acknowledgment action. Notification dismissal and media controls have separate in-app choices.

Kotatsu Action Service is an optional accessibility service for lock-screen overlays and widgets, the clock cover, locking and screensaver features. It is not needed for ordinary launcher preview use. Android access grants and Kotatsu's Sensitive access choices are separate.

Does no Internet permission mean the phone is offline?

No. The launcher has no Internet permission or built-in updater. External apps and providers opened from it, including browsers, document providers and Mudita weather handoffs, can network independently. Android-managed backup and device transfer remain enabled.

Kotatsu Weather declares Internet and network-state access. Its optional location request gets a one-off fix while setup is open, never in the background. Search for a place instead to avoid granting location access.

How do hidden apps and lock-screen notifications work?

Settings → All Apps separates Hidden Apps and Kompakt System Apps. Stock apps omitted by Mudita's launcher, app stores, FUTO Keyboard and Mudita's Update start hidden; show them individually. A hidden app needs its Notify box ticked to appear on the lock screen, and lock-screen exclusions still take precedence. Experimental lock-screen notifications also need Kotatsu Action Service.

Why isn't a notification badge showing or clearing?

Check Settings → Notification Log. Kotatsu reads notifications Android actually has; it cannot show one that never arrived or clear one Android keeps active. Some apps depend on Google Play Services for push delivery. For ordinary app dots, Android's per-app Allow notification dot setting controls whether a dot is allowed.

Can I build it myself?

Yes. Use JDK 21, Android SDK 36.1 and the repository's Gradle wrapper. Run ./gradlew :app:assembleDebug :kotatsu-weather:assembleDebug. See the README build instructions for output paths and release signing.
